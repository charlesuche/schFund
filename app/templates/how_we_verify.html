How we verify scholarships

A wrong deadline or an invented requirement can cost you a scholarship. These are the rules every listing follows.

We only list real scholarships

We leave a scholarship out if it:

  • asks you to pay an agent, consultant or "scholarship service" to apply, to be considered, or to "unlock" the award;
  • asks you to send documents to a personal address such as Gmail or Yahoo instead of the institution's own address;
  • is a lottery, a random draw or an essay contest with no real academic check;
  • has only broken links, dead websites or advert sites behind it.

Normal fees paid directly to a university or an official agency, such as an application or enrolment fee, are not a warning sign. We show them on the scholarship page so you are not surprised.

Official sources first

We read the provider's own website and, where there is one, the full official call for applications. Landing pages often leave out documents, grade rules and deadlines. Blogs and listing sites help us find scholarships, but they never outrank the official source: when they disagree, the official source wins.

Every scholarship page lists the sources we used, with links, so you can check them yourself.

We never fill gaps by guessing

If the official source doesn't say something, we leave it out instead of guessing. Money is shown in the currency the provider uses, without conversion.

Dates are worked out every day

Each page shows whether a scholarship is open, closed or expected to open, based on its dates and today's date. Closed scholarships never show as open, even if we checked them while they were open. When the dates can't tell, the page says the dates are being checked.

A person reviews every listing

Research can be done with the help of software, but nothing is published until a person has reviewed it. Each page shows the day it was last verified.

We keep checking

Every week we check the official pages behind each listing for changes. When a page changes, when a new round is due, or when a listing hasn't been verified for 60 days, a person checks it again.

We never charge students

This site is free. We don't act as an agent and we never ask you for money.

Seen a mistake?

Please report a problem. Every report is read by a person.
